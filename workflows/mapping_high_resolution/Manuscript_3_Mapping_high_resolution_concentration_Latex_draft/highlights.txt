Highlights

- A 51-point network resolved three-dimensional gas heterogeneity in a dairy barn.
- CO2, CH4 and NH3 concentrations increased systematically with sampling height.
- No single sampling point represented all gas concentrations and gas ratios.
- A 34-point network reproduced median dense-network estimates within 2.05%.
- Reduced networks retained episodic NH3 errors relevant to emission estimates.
