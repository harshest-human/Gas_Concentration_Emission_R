# Author confirmations required before submission

The literature-integrated manuscript was written using only information
currently documented in the workflow. The following reproducibility details
should be confirmed by the author before submission rather than inferred.

1. Exact internal and external diameters and lengths of all PTFE sampling lines.
2. Sampling flow rates, pumps, filters, heated-line status, and manifold/MPV
   manufacturers and models.
3. Whether line lengths were equalised among locations and analysers.
4. Time zone and daylight-saving treatment applied to all timestamps.
5. Calibration-gas certificate identifiers, stated uncertainties, traceability,
   test flow rates, and acceptance criteria.
6. Exact analyser firmware/software versions and the manufacturer specification
   used for the stated plus/minus 5% accuracy.
7. Whether the 24-h comparison factors were calculated from paired time points,
   means, regression slopes, or another estimator, including sample size and
   uncertainty.
8. Exact barn orientation, dimensions of permanent openings, curtain states,
   fan locations/ratings and operating logs during Campaigns 1 and 2.
9. Animal occupancy and feeding/milking schedules during the two campaigns.
10. Formal missing-data rule, minimum observations per block, and whether model
    residual diagnostics and multiplicity adjustments will be reported in a
    supplement.
11. Whether all software package versions should be frozen with `renv` for the
    submitted reproducibility archive.
12. Data and code repository accession/DOI wording for the Data Availability
    section.
