## Approved comparison language

Composite lines provide a spatially integrated concentration at the analyser but conceal variability among their contributing inlets. Dedicated lines preserve local identity but require temporal aggregation when many ports are sampled sequentially. Neither architecture is universally superior. Its suitability depends on whether the target quantity is a local concentration, a barn-volume summary or a flow-weighted exhaust concentration.
