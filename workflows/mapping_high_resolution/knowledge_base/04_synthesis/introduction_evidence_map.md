## Current central gap

Long-term field studies provide temporal realism but often use composite sampling lines that physically average several inlets. Dense field studies and controlled models resolve local gradients but generally cover selected locations, shorter periods or prescribed boundary conditions. Manuscript 3 contributes individual-point three-dimensional coverage combined with extended seasonal observations.

## Claims requiring caution

- `No previous study` is stronger than the present audit supports. Retain `to the authors' knowledge` until all priority papers have verified notes.
- Declerck2025 did not isolate sampling height experimentally.
- Janke2022 established a conditional reference under selected stable cross-flow, not a universal reference method.
- A statistically detectable spatial difference is not automatically an operationally important emission error.
