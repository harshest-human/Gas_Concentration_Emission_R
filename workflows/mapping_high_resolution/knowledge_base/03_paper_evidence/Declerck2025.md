## Primary research question

Determine NH3 emissions from traditional naturally ventilated dairy barns and compare an ILVO direct opening-flow method with a VERA-type indirect CO2-balance method.

## Barns and duration

- Four dairy barns in Flanders.
- Each barn was monitored continuously for one year.
- B1: 1 January 2023 to 1 January 2024.
- B2: 1 May 2023 to 1 May 2024.
- B3 and B4: 1 October 2023 to 1 October 2024.
- Source: Section 3.4.1 and Table 2, report page 21.

## Sampling design

- The ILVO gas system sampled composite lines at both side openings and approximately 0.50 m below the ridge.
- The VERA-type internal composite line was positioned 3 m below the ridge. The minimum line height was 6 m above the floor in barn B3.
- Composite-line inlets were installed every 10 m.
- Each inlet used a filtered critical capillary operated at 1.2 L min-1.
- Pumps transported air to the measurement vehicle.
- A multiplexer switched lines every 10 min so that each line was measured once per hour.
- Source: Sections 3.5.1 and 3.5.2, report pages 22--26.

## Direct and indirect methods

- The ILVO method determined opening flow using ultrasonic anemometers distributed vertically at the side openings and additional ridge measurements.
- The VERA-type method calculated ventilation from CO2 production and inside-outside concentration differences.
- The comparison changed several method components simultaneously. It did not isolate sampling height as a single experimental factor.

## Practical interpretation

The study supplies strong long-term multi-barn evidence that complete measurement methods can produce barn- and flow-dependent differences. Composite lines improve operational spatial integration but do not retain the identity of individual inlets.
