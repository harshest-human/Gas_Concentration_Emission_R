## Primary research question

Compare two indirect CO2-balance sampling strategies with airflow measured directly at a barn opening under selected stable cross-flow conditions.

## Barn and measurement period

- Naturally ventilated dairy barn near Rostock, Germany.
- Barn dimensions: 96.15 m by 34.2 m.
- Measurement period: September 2017 to February 2018.
- Analysis used prevailing southern winds to approximate stable cross-flow.
- Source: Sections 2.1, 2.2 and 2.6, paper pages 3--6.

## Sampling design

- Two outside and four inside composite PTFE sampling lines.
- Internal tube diameter: 6 mm.
- Critical orifices at 8--10 m intervals.
- Gas analyser: Gasmet CX4000 FTIR with a manifold.
- Each line: 10 min comprising 4 min flushing and 6 min measurement.
- All six lines were sampled sequentially once per hour.
- Direct method: nine ultrasonic anemometers across the southern opening.
- Source: Section 2.2, Figure 2, paper pages 4--5.

## Indirect configurations

- SM1 was wind-related and used the windward outside line and the leeward inside line under selected southern cross-flow.
- SM2 used an averaged internal concentration and the minimum measured outside concentration.
- These were configuration-specific estimands rather than interchangeable measurements of one perfectly mixed concentration.

## Statistical analysis

- Tukey ladder-of-powers transformation for skewed volume-flow values.
- Pearson correlations and correlograms.
- Regression and bias-adjustment comparisons.
- Direct opening flow was treated as a conditional reference during stable cross-flow.

Despite the slightly higher unadjusted correlation for SM2, the authors concluded that wind-related SM1 was closer to the direct method in stable cross-inflow conditions after considering bias, spread and the physical sampling pathway. Source: Discussion and Conclusions, paper pages 10--12.

## Mechanistic interpretation

SM2 included lines influenced by air close to the inlet. This lowered the averaged internal CO2 concentration, reduced the inside-outside difference and could overestimate CO2-balance ventilation. SM1 could also fail under buoyancy-dominated conditions if the chosen leeward line did not intercept the principal outgoing flow.

## Limitations

- Results were restricted to selected stable southern cross-flow.
- The period covered autumn and winter rather than a complete year.
- The direct method itself retained uncertainty from anemometer placement and wind-break nets.
- Findings do not establish one universal sampling strategy for all weather conditions.
