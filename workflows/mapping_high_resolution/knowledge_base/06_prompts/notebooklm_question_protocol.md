# Targeted NotebookLM and Gemini question protocol

Answers must identify the exact primary source and page, table or figure where possible. Reported facts must be separated from interpretation.

## Inventory of sampling-focused studies

1. Which uploaded studies treated spatial or temporal gas-sampling design in naturally ventilated dairy or cattle barns as a primary research aim?
2. For each study, report barn type and dimensions, animal number, gases and tracers, analyser, sampling duration, raw and aggregated resolution, SP number, heights, horizontal coverage and functional zones.
3. Which studies measured below 2.6 m around lying cubicles, manure alleys or the animal-occupied zone?
4. Which studies sampled near the ridge or roof, and what remained unresolved?

## Quantitative findings

5. What vertical and horizontal concentration differences, CVs, gradients, errors or mixing-ratio variations were reported for CO2, CH4 and NH3?
6. Which studies quantified reduced sampling density? Report the tested densities and resulting bias or uncertainty.
7. Under which boundary conditions was one point, one height or an approximately 3 m height representative or unrepresentative?
8. Which studies quantified emission or ventilation error from unresolved concentration or velocity fields?

## Mechanisms and practical constraints

9. Which mechanisms were proposed for spatial heterogeneity?
10. Which practical constraints shaped sampling designs, including animal damage, tractor access, tubing, flushing, multiplexing, analyser cost and port number?
11. Separate evidence-based sampling recommendations from speculation.

## Statistics and inference

12. For each central paper, list the statistical tests, experimental unit, temporal aggregation, repeated structure, autocorrelation handling and multiplicity correction.
13. Identify conclusions limited by one barn, short campaigns, non-simultaneous sampling, stable-wind filtering or confounding of season and design.

## Manuscript-specific synthesis

14. Contrast long-term composite-line studies with individual-point spatial studies.
15. Identify which previous findings can be compared directly with a 17 by 3 network and which cannot.
16. Identify claims requiring a new primary-source search.

NotebookLM output is a discovery aid. Transfer a claim to the manuscript only after primary-source verification and entry into `03_paper_evidence/` and `05_claim_audit/`.
