# Manuscript 3 study-design facts

Status: approved project facts unless marked otherwise.

## Barn and network

- Naturally ventilated dairy barn at LVAT Gross Kreutz, Brandenburg, Germany.
- Nominal capacity: 58 dairy cows.
- Barn dimensions used in the manuscript: 38.88 m by 17.65 m.
- Campaign 1 internal network: 51 individual SPs.
- Horizontal arrangement: 17 positions.
- Vertical arrangement: top, middle and bottom at every horizontal position.
- Top SP: 0.60 m below the local sloping roof.
- Middle SP: 3.60 m above the floor.
- Bottom SP: 2.60 m above the floor.
- Each SP had a dedicated PTFE branch line. SPs were not physically averaged before analysis.

## SP numbering

- Top: 1, 4, 7, ..., 49.
- Middle: 2, 5, 8, ..., 50.
- Bottom: 3, 6, 9, ..., 51.
- `s`: outside south reference where available.
- `ring_in`: ring-line identifier used in later campaigns. It is not part of Manuscript 3 Campaigns 1 and 2.

## Campaign 1 allocation

- CRDS9: SP1--SP16.
- FTIR2: SP17--SP26.
- CRDS8: SP27--SP42.
- FTIR1: SP43--SP51 and the outside line.
- FTIR harmonisation was derived from the preliminary 24 h co-located comparison.

## Campaign 2 interpretation

- Campaign 2 used two CRDS analysers and 32 ports.
- It retained top and bottom locations after FTIR unavailability.
- It is a seasonal extension and not a full replication of the 51-SP design.
- SP19 and SP40 remain eligible where valid data are available.
- Campaign 2 is used for concentration and spatial-pattern analyses. It is not used for comparative emissions because an adequate contemporaneous outside series is unavailable.

## Wording restrictions

- Do not state that middle SPs were removed because the data proved they were unnecessary.
- Do not call the outside reference SP52 in the manuscript narrative.
- Do not describe the two-hour block as the raw sampling resolution.
- Do not claim that a reduced concentration network has been validated for emission measurement without matched airflow measurements.
