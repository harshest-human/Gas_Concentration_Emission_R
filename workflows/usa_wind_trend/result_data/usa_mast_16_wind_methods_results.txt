Materials and Methods

Local wind conditions at the LVAT barn site were characterized with an ultrasonic anemometer (USA, WindMaster Pro, Gill Instruments Ltd., Lymington, Hampshire, UK) mounted on a mast approximately 10 m above ground level and about 100 m east of the investigated barn. The sensor records the orthogonal wind components u, v, and w, representing the west-east, south-north, and vertical velocity components, respectively. For the present analysis, the raw USA_mast_16 component data were aggregated to hourly mean components in local time (Europe/Berlin). Horizontal wind speed was calculated as ws = sqrt(u^2 + v^2), and meteorological wind direction was derived from the hourly mean u and v components as the direction from which the air flow originated. The vertical component w was retained in the processed time series but was not used for windrose generation because standard windrose statistics are based on horizontal flow only. This approach follows the WindMaster instrument description, which reports horizontal wind speed in the UV plane and the vertical W component separately, together with standard meteorological conversion practice for deriving wind direction from horizontal wind components (Gill Instruments WindMaster Pro manual; NCAR/EOL wind-direction reference).

Results

The processed hourly record covered 01.01.2024 to 25.08.2025. For historical interpretation, wind conditions were summarized from summer 2024 to spring 2025, ending on 07.04.2025 before the experimental measurement week. Across this historical period (01.06.2024 to 07.04.2025), the mean hourly wind speed was 1.47 m s^-1 and the speed-weighted prevailing direction was SE. The most frequent directional sectors were SE (29.5%), E (16.8%), S (12.0%).

Summer 2024 (01.06.2024 to 31.08.2024): mean hourly wind speed 0.97 m s^-1; prevailing direction SE; most frequent sectors SE (28.3%), E (16.7%), S (13.8%). Autumn 2024 (01.09.2024 to 30.11.2024): mean hourly wind speed 1.31 m s^-1; prevailing direction SE; most frequent sectors SE (29.4%), W (14.3%), S (13.6%). Winter 2024/25 (01.12.2024 to 28.02.2025): mean hourly wind speed 2.42 m s^-1; prevailing direction SE; most frequent sectors SE (39.3%), E (22.8%), S (10.7%). Spring 2025 (01.03.2025 to 07.04.2025): mean hourly wind speed 1.52 m s^-1; prevailing direction N; most frequent sectors NW (18.7%), N (17.4%), W (14.4%).

During the measurement week (08.04.2025 to 15.04.2025), the mean hourly wind speed was 1.87 m s^-1 and the speed-weighted prevailing direction was E. The most frequent directional sectors were E (34.8%), W (16.8%), NE (14.7%). Overall, the measurement week remained within the local directional envelope of the historical record while showing distinct day-to-day shifts in sector dominance, as illustrated by the faceted daily windroses.
